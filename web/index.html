    AC auto recovery

    When enabled, an unexpected AC-off state triggers an AC-on command. A detected inverter trip may briefly cycle AC off and back on. This setting applies only to the selected Jackery. It starts on for the Explorer 5000 Plus and off for other models.

      Excess diversion

      When the forecast says you have headroom — predicted sunrise SOC comfortably above your target — this dumps the excess energy into a downstream load (e.g. an EV charger plugged into the Jackery's AC output) instead of letting it curtail at 100%. House load always wins: the controller stops the diversion the moment the projection would put sunrise at the safety floor, leaving the battery preserved for your normal usage. Solar is the typical source that creates the surplus, but the gate is forecast-driven — it'll divert overnight too if the next day's predicted solar refills the buffer. Test mode logs decisions but doesn't toggle the plug — use it for a few days to verify the controller's calls before flipping to active.

        Backup & restore

        — ▾

        Daily 1-shot backup of the SQLite database and every credential / settings file in /data to a remote NAS, over either SMB or rsync. The encryption key itself stays on this device — restoring on a fresh install will preserve all telemetry and let you sign in with your existing credentials, but device-side secrets (Kasa, Anthropic/OpenAI, etc.) get re-prompted. rsync over SSH adds delta transfer + hardlinked snapshots, so year-long retention costs near-zero extra disk on the NAS.

        Found on your network Scanning…

          Note: The rsyncd protocol has no remote-delete primitive, so automatic retention is a no-op for this transport. Old snapshots accumulate unless you prune them manually on the NAS, or switch to rsync over SSH (path, key auth) against a server that supports it (TrueNAS, Linux, older Synology DSM).
